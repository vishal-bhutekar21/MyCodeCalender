# MyCodeCalendar: Contests & DSA

## Short Description (75 characters):
Track live coding contests across LeetCode, Codeforces, CodeChef & AtCoder.

## Full Description:
MyCodeCalendar is the ultimate companion app for competitive programmers, computer science students, and software engineers. Never miss an upcoming coding competition or hackathon again!

Track upcoming and live algorithmic contests in real time across the world’s top competitive programming platforms with automated push alerts, Google Calendar sync, and curated DSA practice sheets.
